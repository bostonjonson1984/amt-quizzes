Block 5 · Rivet Head Markings

Learn to READ solid-rivet head marks and the alloy code behind them: plain 1100 (A), recessed-dot 2117 (AD, the field rivet), raised-dot 2017 (D), two-raised-dashes 2024 (DD), raised-cross 5056 (B, for magnesium), and raised-ring 7050 (E). Covers which are ice-box rivets and why, the MS part-number alloy code, and the 'more marks = stronger' logic. Every answer verified against FAA-H-8083-31B Ch. 4 (Fig. 4-77) and cited.
