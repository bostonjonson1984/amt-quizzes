Block 5 · Rivet Part Numbers

Learn to decode an MS/AN solid-rivet part number piece by piece — e.g. MS20470AD4-4 = universal head, 2117 alloy, 1/8" diameter, 1/4" length. Covers the head-style number (20470 universal, 20426 countersunk), the alloy letters (A/AD/D/DD/B, and no-letter = mild steel), diameter in 32nds, length in 16ths, half sizes, full worked decodes, and how the code letters match the head marking. Verified against FAA-H-8083-30B Ch. 7, FAA-H-8083-31B Ch. 4, and AC 43.13-1B, each answer cited.
Tip: press A–D (or 1–4) to answer, Enter for next.
